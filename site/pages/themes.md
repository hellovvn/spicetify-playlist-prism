# Playlist themes

<p class="lead">Nine moving skies across Spotify, and a different one per playlist.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/themes.webp" alt="A playlist page switching between Prism themes">

## Pick a theme

Choose one in [Settings](settings.html) &gt; **Playlist theme**:

**Twilight**, **Northern lights**, **Starry night**, **Milky Way**, **Bubble nebula**,
**Afterglow**, **Misty morning**, **Blossom canopy** and **Window rain**.

## What changes

- Playlist pages get a moving sky, a colour rail beside the songs (each song's main genre) and a
  genre mix bar.
- Albums, artists, Liked Songs, Home, Search and the other pages get the same sky or a quiet dark purple.
- Spotify's green turns pastel and its bars turn to frosted glass.
- Lyrics get the sky too, with the player controls always there.

## One playlist, its own theme

Right-click a playlist and choose **Prism theme**. That's saved only on your computer and never
changes the playlist.
