# Stats beside your music

<p class="lead">A playlist's stats right next to it while you listen.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/side-panel.webp" alt="Prism stats in Spotify's side panel next to a playlist">

Open any playlist and Spotify's right-hand panel gets a **Prism stats** tab next to **Now playing**.
It fills in songs it hasn't scanned yet while you look. **View full stats** opens the whole
[stats page](stats.html).

Turn it off in [Settings](settings.html) with **Prism stats in the side panel**.
