# Playlist tone

<p class="lead">Give a playlist a vibe and see which songs fit it and which drift.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/tone.webp" alt="Score rings showing how well each song fits a playlist's tone">

## Step by step

1. Right-click a playlist and choose **Add Prism tone**.
2. Pick its key genres, lyrics moods and tempo. Prism suggests a start from the songs already in it.
3. A **Tone** strip appears on the playlist page. Switch it on to see a score ring on every song.

Songs under 50% get a grey ring, so the drifters stand out. Songs without Last.fm tags are scored on
mood and tempo only, shown as a faded ring.

Playlists you [grow](grow.html) with Prism start with a tone. Tones are saved only on your
computer and never change the playlist.
