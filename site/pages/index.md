# Prism

<p class="lead">A Spotify companion that helps you get more out of the music you already have.</p>

Grow a playlist from a favourite song, remix messy playlists into tidy ones, see what each playlist
is made of, and give Spotify a softer, twilight look. Prism is a free
[Spicetify](https://spicetify.app) app for the Spotify desktop app.

## Your music, judged song by song

Prism works from your own library and looks at each song on its own: its Last.fm genre and mood
tags, its tempo and key, and the mood of its lyrics, not just the artist's genre.
[How songs are matched](how-it-works.html).

It only writes to Spotify when you click a button that says so, and it never deletes playlists or
removes songs. [What Prism changes](what-prism-changes.html).
