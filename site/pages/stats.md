# Playlist stats

<p class="lead">See what any playlist is made of.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/stats.webp" alt="Playlist stats page with stat cards, genre bars and fingerprint bubbles">

## Open it

Right-click a playlist and choose **Prism stats**.

## What it shows

- Songs, length, average tempo, top mood and tone fit
- The **genre mix** as glass bars
- Mood mix and tempo spread (slowest and fastest)
- Artists, eras (songs per decade) and key (major or minor)
- A **fingerprint** of glass bubbles, one per genre. Pop them.

For playlists Prism made, it also shows the seed song or source playlists and how close each song
is to the seed. Other playlists get an estimate from what's already been scanned.
