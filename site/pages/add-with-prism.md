# Add with Prism

<p class="lead">Let Prism find the best home for a song.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/add-with-prism.webp" alt="Add with Prism showing the five best-fitting playlists for a song">

## Step by step

1. Right-click any song, including the one playing in the player bar, and choose **Add with Prism**.
2. The menu shows the five playlists it fits best.
3. Click one to add the song to its end.

**More on the Prism page…** shows the scores and the shared genres. You can also start from the
Prism page with **Add a song to your playlists**.

Playlists are judged on genre, lyrics mood, tempo and key. Only playlists you can add to are
suggested, and never ones that already have the song.
