# Remix into moods

<p class="lead">Shuffle your playlists into fresh ones sorted by genre and mood. Make several, or just the one you want.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/remix.webp" alt="Remixing a playlist into groups by genre and mood">

## Step by step

Pick one or more playlists on the Prism page. After the scan, choose a door:

- **Sort them for me:** Prism suggests groups by genre, mood and tempo, and names them.
- **I'll pick the vibes:** you choose the genres and moods for each group, and Prism sorts the songs in.

Each group is a card with an album-art mosaic. Click one to rename it, move or remove songs, or
combine it with another group.

## More options

- **How many groups:** Auto, or 2 to 8
- **Group mostly by:** Balanced, Genre, Mood or Energy (tempo)
- **Overlap:** whether a song may land in more than one group

Changing one regroups at once.

## Not included

Songs that fit nowhere wait on a dashed **Not included** card, with the reason for each. Add them
one by one, or all at once into the closest group.

**Create playlists** makes one new playlist per group. Your original playlists are never changed.
