# Grow a playlist

<p class="lead">Start with one song you love and get a playlist of songs that feel like it.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/grow.webp" alt="Growing a playlist from one song: ranked matches with score rings and New finds">

## Start

Right-click any song and choose **Start a Prism playlist**. You can also start from the Prism page
with the song that's playing, or a pasted song link.

## Step by step

1. Prism shows the song's genre tags, tempo, key and lyrics mood. Switch off any tag that doesn't fit.
2. Pick where songs can come from: any of your playlists and/or Liked Songs.
3. Prism ranks every song with a score ring, the shared tags and why it matched.
4. Keep 10, 20, 30, 50, all, or your own number.
5. **New finds** (Off, 5, 10 or 20): similar songs from Last.fm that aren't in your library yet.
   They're marked in mint and only go into the new playlist.
6. Click **Create**. The seed song comes first and the rest is shuffled, with new finds spread through it.

> A grown playlist starts with a [tone](tone.html) made from the tags you kept, so later you can
> see which songs drift from it.
