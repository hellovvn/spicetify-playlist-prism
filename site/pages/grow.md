# Grow a playlist

<p class="lead">Start with one song you love, or a whole playlist, and get more songs that feel like it.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/grow.webp" alt="Growing a playlist from one song: picking its tags, choosing how many new finds, then ranked matches with score rings">

## Start

Right-click any song and choose **Grow a playlist with Prism**. You can also start from the Prism page
with the song that's playing, or a pasted song link.

To grow from a **whole playlist**, right-click it and choose **Grow from this playlist**, or choose
**One of your playlists** on the Prism page's Grow screen and tick the one you want.

## Step by step

1. Prism shows the song's genre tags, tempo, key and lyrics mood. Switch off any tag that doesn't fit.
2. Choose how many **new finds** you want (Off, 5, 10, 20, or your own number up to 50): similar songs from Last.fm that aren't
   in your library yet. They're marked in mint and only go into the new playlist.
3. Pick where your own songs can come from: any of your playlists and/or Liked Songs. Or click
   **Skip** to get only new finds (not possible with new finds Off).
4. Prism scans the playlists and ranks every song with a score ring, the shared tags and why it matched.
5. Keep 10, 20, 30, 50, all, or your own number. You can still change the new finds count here.
6. Click **Create**. The seed song comes first and the rest is shuffled, with new finds spread through it.

## Growing from a playlist

Prism first reads the playlist's songs (songs it scanned before load at once). Instead of one
song's tags you see every genre in the playlist, with a bar for how many of its songs have it.
Switch off genres you don't want more of. Under **Show seed songs** you can untick songs that
don't fit; they're left out of the new playlist and don't count towards the genres.

A song is matched on the genres you kept. Its tempo, key and lyrics mood are compared with the
seed songs most like it, so a playlist of slow R&B and fast rock still finds slow R&B and fast
rock (not mid-tempo songs that match neither). The seed playlist itself isn't offered as a
source, and its songs never show up as matches.

The new playlist starts with the seed playlist's songs in their own order, followed by the new
picks, shuffled. The seed playlist isn't changed.

> A grown playlist starts with a [tone](tone.html) made from the tags you kept, so later you can
> see which songs drift from it.
