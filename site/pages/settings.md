# Settings

<p class="lead">Open them from the Prism page, or from Spotify's profile menu &gt; <b>Prism settings</b>.</p>

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Your own Last.fm API key** | Optional. Without one, Prism uses its shared key. [How to get one](lastfm-key.html). |
| **Playlist theme** | No theme, or one of the [nine skies](themes.html). |
| **Prism look for lyrics** | On or off. Off brings back Spotify's own lyrics colours. |
| **Prism stats in the side panel** | On or off. See [Stats beside your music](side-panel.html). |

Settings also has **About** (the Last.fm credit and a Support Prism button) and **Help make Prism
better**, where you can report a bug or suggest an idea. A report becomes a public issue on GitHub,
and you can see and untick the details it sends.
