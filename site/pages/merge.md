# Merge playlists

<p class="lead">Stack your playlists together and drop the doubles.</p>

<img class="clip" src="media/merge.svg" alt="Two playlist bubbles drifting into one, with the doubles dropped">

## Step by step

1. On the Prism page, click **Merge playlists** and pick two or more playlists.
2. Prism shows how many songs each one has, the total, and how many duplicates it drops. The same
   song from another album counts as a double too.
3. **Show duplicates** lists every copy left out, and which copy was kept.
4. Name the new playlist and click **Merge**.

## Rename the originals

If you leave **Rename the originals** on, your own originals get "[merged] " in front of their
name, so you can tell them apart. Their songs, order, cover and description stay as they are.
Playlists you follow and Liked Songs are never renamed.
